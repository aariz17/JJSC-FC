Our Roots & Brotherhood

Born in Gopalgonj, Built to Conquer

JJSC FC (JhonJhonia Football Club) was founded in Gopalgonj with a singular mission: to unite the sharpest local footballing talent under one badge of honor. From high-intensity floodlight turf clashes to regional knockout tournaments across Gopalgonj District, our squad plays with fearless pressing, quick one-touch combinations, and unbreakable unity.
